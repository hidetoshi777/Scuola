Inglese · Scheda

Excuse me, where is…?

Nome Classe Data

1I posti della città. Scrivi il nome in inglese.

church · pharmacy · bank · hospital · supermarket · school · post office · traffic lights

2Frecce e frasi. Scrivi la lettera giusta nel quadratino.

  1. a. Turn right.
  2. b. Go straight on.
  3. c. Turn left.

3Traduci in italiano.

  1. Excuse me, where is the bank?
  2. Take the second right.
  3. It’s opposite the cinema.
  4. Thank you! — You’re welcome!

4Parti dalla stazione (YOU ARE HERE). Segui le indicazioni. Dove arrivi?

  1. Take the first right. It’s on the left, opposite the hotel.
    → the
  2. Take the second right. It’s on the left, opposite the pharmacy.
    → the
  3. Take the first left. It’s on the right, opposite the café.
    → the
  4. Go straight on to the end of the road. Turn left. It’s on the right, opposite the church.
    → the

5Completa con: opposite · next · Excuse · straight · welcome

  1. me, where is the hotel?
  2. Go on.
  3. The bank is the cinema. (di fronte)
  4. The bakery is to the bank. (accanto)
  5. Thank you! — You’re !
